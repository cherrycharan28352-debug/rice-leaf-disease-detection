# Privacy Policy for RiceCare AI — Rice Leaf Disease Detection

**Last Updated:** October 2026

## 1. Introduction
RiceCare AI ("we", "our", or "us") provides an AI-powered agricultural mobile and web application designed to help farmers, agronomists, and researchers identify plant diseases on rice leaves. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed through our application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Photographs and Images:** When you use the disease detection feature, the app captures or uploads images of rice leaves to perform botanical classification and lesion severity analysis. Images are processed solely to return diagnostic results.
- **Device & Camera Permissions:** We request camera and storage permissions only when you take a photo or select an image from your gallery. We do not access contacts, microphones, or location without your explicit consent.
- **Optional Account Information:** Basic diagnosis is available without an account (Guest Mode). If you register, we store your name, username, email, and encrypted password hash (`scrypt`).
- **Diagnosis History:** If authenticated, past analysis records (predicted condition, confidence score, date) are stored in your account so you can review them. You can permanently delete any history record at any time.

---

## 3. How We Use Information
- To execute neural inference with the RiceNet/Quantum VQC model.
- To compute lesion area percentages and provide crop management advice.
- To display your personal diagnosis history.
- We **never** sell, rent, or monetize your images or personal data.

---

## 4. Data Safety & Security
- All communications between the mobile application and our backend use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.
- Passwords are salted and hashed using cryptographic algorithms.
- Images are stored securely and can be removed by the user at any time.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services
RiceCare AI does not share personal user data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 6. Agricultural Advisory Disclaimer
RiceCare AI predictions and crop management advice are computational estimates to assist field scouting. Always consult a local agricultural extension officer or certified agronomist before applying chemical treatments.
